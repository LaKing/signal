# Changelog

All notable changes to Signal are recorded here, by milestone (`docs/roadmap.md`). The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Versions are `MAJOR.MINOR.N`, where `N` counts the commits since the tag `vMAJOR.MINOR.0` (`scripts/version.sh`); a section covers a `MAJOR.MINOR` line.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **Snapshot transition time** (ADR 0041 §10, the owner's request of 2026-10-25). A restore or undo can move the controls to their values over a time instead of at once, against clicks and jumps: the Snapshots card's Transition field (ms, kept as `transition_ms` in `snapshots.toml`); continuous controls ramp linearly from where they are (a restore mid-transition starts from the running ramp), stepped ones (small modes and selectors, gates) switch at the start, and the file holds the targets at once. `snapshot.transition` and `transitionMs` on `snapshot.restore`/`snapshot.undo` in the UI protocol, `snapshot_transition` and `transitionMs` in `signal mcp`, `signal snapshot transition`.
- **Snapshots in your order** (ADR 0041 §9, the owner's request of 2026-10-25). The Snapshots card's rows can be dragged by their ⋮⋮ handle (a line shows where the row lands; Escape cancels) or moved one place with Alt+↑ and Alt+↓ on a focused row; the order is kept as `order = [...]` in `snapshots.toml`, new snapshots go to the end, and lists come in that order. `snapshot.move` in the UI protocol, `snapshot_move` in `signal mcp`, `signal snapshot move` on the command line.
- **Snapshots** (ADR 0041, the owner's request of 2026-10-24). A project keeps named snapshots of its parameters in `snapshots.toml`: Save records every top-level parameter's value as it plays (an unsaved live edit included), Restore writes the snapshot's values back into `project.toml` as one edit and plays them as a parameter edit does (gone parameters skipped and named, out-of-range values clamped and named), Delete removes one. In Signal.app a Snapshots card in the Parameters tab (a name field with Save, Restore and Delete per snapshot; Replace and Delete ask first); in the UI protocol `snapshot.list`, `snapshot.save`, `snapshot.restore`, `snapshot.delete`; in `signal mcp` the tools `snapshot_list`, `snapshot_save`, `snapshot_restore` (checked by the gain guard) and `snapshot_delete`; on the command line `signal snapshot list|save|restore|delete`. A control can be left out of snapshots in its properties (the Parameters tab's "snapshots" box: `snapshot_exclude = ["run"]` in its instance's entry), and every restore first keeps the values it replaces as an undo snapshot: "Undo restore" brings them back, and pressing it again redoes (`snapshot.undo`, `snapshot_undo`, `signal snapshot undo`). Names are any text up to 64 characters; the file is byte-stable, written under the project's writer lock (a change by another writer is a conflict, never lost), and packed into `.signal` files. Nothing that plays or renders reads it.

- **`std.tanh_shaper_os8`** (M3, the "ADAA versus oversampling" experiment, `experiments/m3-tanh-aliasing/`; verdict of 2026-10-23, the choice delegated by the owner). `tanh` saturation at 8× the rate with first-order ADAA, through linear-phase FIR resampling: the same ports and `drive` parameter as `std.tanh_shaper`, so a project switches by the type name. Its inharmonic energy at drive 20 and 96 kHz is 101.6–141.2 dB below the fundamental on V6's tones (`std.tanh_shaper`: 22.0–49.0 dB), and its passband is flat to −0.03 dB at 10 kHz at 48 kHz. It costs 73.8125 frames of delay at every rate and about 4.5 times `std.tanh_shaper`'s CPU, and its output overshoots 1 on clipped high tones (up to about 1.27), so `out` declares no range. V6 now applies to it and passes (the threshold unchanged); `std.tanh_shaper`, every project and every golden are unchanged. It was `lab.tanh_os8_adaa1`, which leaves the lab; the other four candidates stay there, built on its resampling (`signal_std::oversample`).
- **M3.2 Projects as content and slots, phase 1 (offline)** (ADR 0035 and ADR 0037, the owner's direction of 2026-10-23). A whole project can be an instance of another: `drums = { project = "../studio-transport" }`, read and checked by every rule in its own folder, with its problems located in its files. Inside the host its `std.audio_out` is planned as the new `std.audio_tap` (`Registry::set_sink_tap`), whose outputs `l_out`, `r_out` are, as numbers, what the project records alone; a project without `[exports] outputs` exports its sink's channels `l`, `r`, and `project.toml` may now carry `[exports]` (checked, meaning nothing alone). An include is a **scope root**: it draws the random streams it draws alone, under its own seed or the entry's `seed`, whatever the host's seed or the include's name (`PlanInstance::stream`, in the engine and the oracle). `set` overrides an include's top-level parameters; an include runs at its host's rate and its own is noted (`signal render --json` `notes`, `describe`'s `content`); cycles are refused naming the chain, and includes nest 8 deep at most. **Slots**: an instance with its own interface (inline, from `interfaces/<name>.toml`, or from the library templates `lib.voice`, `lib.instrument` and `lib.deck`, with `from`, `remove` and retyping entries) and an optional `load` (a module or a project), matched by exact name with `map`, and planned byte-identically to the explicit instance with the wires renamed (`signal_graph::ports_of`); an empty slot plans nothing; the wires on a slot are checked against its interface, loaded or not. The manifest gains the include files (`include:<key>/<path>`), `included_projects` and `library_interface_hashes`, each absent without them, so every existing golden is unchanged. New project `projects/two-studios` (the studio and a copy of studio-transport, mixed by `std.mix`) and its golden `tests/golden/two-studios/`. Live joins, panels and packing of includes are the later phases.
- **M2.15 Keyboard and scrolling** (ADR 0034, owner's request of 2026-10-23). One keymap as text: `ui/lib/keys.default.js` lists every keyboard shortcut and wheel gesture by command and scope, and the controls, the native menu and the docs read it. The person's overrides go in `keys.toml` beside the Setup (written at once with comments kept, watched: an edit elsewhere applies live; problems and conflicts reported with their line and column, a broken file falls back to the built-in keys with a notice). Signal ▸ **Settings…** (⌘,) opens a Settings window whose Keyboard Shortcuts page lists every command, searchable, records a chord by pressing it, adds, removes and resets chords, and says a collision inline; **Help ▸ Keyboard Shortcuts** (⌘/) shows them as a reference. Protocol: `keys.get`, `keys.set`, `keys.reset`, pushed `keys`.
- **M3.1 Transport as a signal** (ADR 0033, owner's direction of 2026-10-22). `std.playhead`: a position in beats on a signal wire, with `bpm`, `run`, `rate` (a multiplier of the tempo: 0 holds, negative plays backwards; a connected signal replaces it) and a `seek` input, computed from the last change of tempo, rate or run so that it never drifts, and summed with compensation while the rate changes on every frame. `std.note_seq` and `std.step_seq8` take a `follow` input: connected to a position, they play from it instead of counting ticks, exact in beats, a stopped transport releasing its note and a jump firing nothing until the position moves on. `lib.transport` in the built-in library, and `projects/studio-transport`, the studio played from its own Transport card (play, stop, tempo, rate, rewind).
- Signal.app: **Project ▸ Restart from Files** plays the open project again from frame 0 from its files.
- A panel binds the exported parameters of a built-in library composite (`lib.*`), as it binds a project composite's.
- The module contract: a primitive can ask which of its inputs the plan connects (`Inputs::is_connected`), and a library can make two inputs exclusive (`Registry::exclusive_inputs`); a project that connects both is refused with the connection named.

### Changed

- The wheel scrolls the page: a knob, a fader, the piano roll or a step grid's level takes it only once clicked or focused (until the focus leaves it or Escape), while ⌥ is held, or when it fills its window (the schematic); a gesture keeps going where it started, and a one-line hint says how to use the wheel inside a control. Page Up, Page Down, Space, Home and End scroll the shown tab or panel wherever no focused control takes them (a focused piano roll scrolls its keys, the schematic pans). The piano roll zooms its keys with ⇧⌘+wheel (was ⌥+wheel); a toggle no longer takes Page Up, Page Down, Home or End; Interface Setup… is ⌥⌘, (⌘, opens Settings); the schematic fits with Home (0 still goes out to the top level).
- Joining the device without a glitch (ADR 0036, the owner's report and decision of 2026-10-23): **the device's clock is the run's.** A live run plays at the rate the output device runs at and Signal never switches a device's sample rate (the switch before the stream, from the previous change, is gone); a project's `sample_rate` is the rate of its renders and replays, and when the device runs at another one Signal plays at the device's and says so ("'SSL 2+ Mk II' runs at 44100 Hz; studio is written for 48000 Hz and plays at 44100 Hz"). The session records the rate played, so its replay is exact. A device whose rate changes while playing stops the run, which plays again at the new rate. Signal no longer sets the buffer of an output or input device that runs for another app: it plays at that device's own buffer; alone on a device it requests the Setup's buffer as before, and `buffer = "device"` in the Setup follows the device's buffer always. Signal's output fades in over 10 ms when it starts and out over 10 ms before it stops, so neither edge clicks (real time only: renders are unchanged). `signal play` reports the buffer it runs at, and `--json` how it joined the device (`device`).
- Typed values (the owner's, 2026-10-22: double-click a number to type it, with a decimal comma or dot): the double-click on a control's value text is told from the two presses (500 ms, 6 px), not only from the browser's `dblclick`, and its presses no longer move or write the value before the field opens (a press on a fader's or knob's readout drags only past 4 px). The scope's trigger level takes a typed value. The piano roll's loop length and Signal.app's Setup buffer and CPU workers read a decimal comma as a dot (text fields instead of WebKit's number field, which dropped it).
- Signal.app's toolbar has no Play or Stop: the open project plays while the power is on (when the app starts, when a project opens, when the power comes on) and starts and stops from its own panel. In Signal.app, `transport.play` while playing plays the project again from frame 0. To list the devices again, switch the power off.

### Fixed

- Audio devices at another sample rate (the owner's incident of 2026-10-22: Signal.app said only "the audio device failed: Unknown property" while the default output ran at another rate than the project's 48 kHz): a device that does not start says which rates in plain words and what to do ("the device 'SSL 2+ Mk II' runs at 44100 Hz and could not be switched to 48000 Hz (CoreAudio: unknown property); set it to 48000 Hz in Audio MIDI Setup, or set sample_rate = 44100 in project.toml"). Opening a device at another rate switches it first with a stream built and dropped unplayed: a stream built across the switch was stopped by the switch's own notification and never called back (`signal play` waited forever; measured on BlackHole 64ch). A stream the audio system stops while playing (the device's rate changed, it went away) is no longer counted as an xrun only: `signal play`, `signal ui` and `signal mcp` end with the reason and a non-zero exit, writing what they recorded. In Signal.app the run stops with the reason, and while switched on it plays again from frame 0 once the output runs at the project's rate (the device list is read again once a second while it waits), or once when the output was already at that rate or the project's files change. `setup.check` takes a device by identity (the dialog sends it), so a device list read again meanwhile cannot make it check another device. `signal devices --json` and `setup.devices` give each device's current `rate`.
- The schematic (the operator's review of 2026-10-22): a frame's pins' names that would leave the drawing area at deep zoom (past its left edge, by the sidebar) go on their pins' other side again, inside the frame, as `docs/ui-controls.md` says (lost when labels moved into the level's units); an open composite's box names a noted pin whole outside its edge, no longer cut to half its box (latent: `flipped` kept a width meant for inside the box); a frame's parameter that nothing outside connects to has no `nothing → cutoff` note; wires that passed under an open composite's frame (the studio's drum triggers under an open voice's corner) go around it; the frame's close × is at least 20 px square on screen, never over the frame's title.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-22

The first public release (M2.14, ADR 0031): Signal plays in real time, edits live, runs as a Mac app with a web UI, and has a schematic, a built-in library, patterns and an agent interface.

### Added

- **M2 Real time and live edits** (ADR 0006, 0011). CoreAudio output through cpal behind a driver trait; plan swaps for live edits and `signal play --watch`, which applies saved project files while it plays; session logs and `signal replay`, which renders a recorded live session again bit for bit; fault guards around primitives; benchmarks (`signal bench`, `docs/benchmarks.md`). The core primitive library: oscillators, filters, envelopes, delay, utility and event primitives. Verified by V9–V11 and V13, and a 30-minute soak at 96 kHz with 0 xruns (V12).
- **M2.5 Signal.app shell** (ADR 0013). A signed `Signal.app` with an interface setup dialog (devices, inputs and outputs, sample rate, buffer) kept as the machine Setup, input and output meters, a parameter panel, and `./start.sh`.
- **M2.6 Multi-core engine** (ADR 0019). Block execution and a deterministic multi-core scheduler: the output is identical for every block size and thread count. Real-time worker threads run in the audio workgroup. `signal-gpu` and GPU-backed modules with declared latency (a lab proof on Metal).
- **M2.7 Full hardware use** (ADR 0019, 0022). Switchable wake policies measured on the device (park50 is the default), task-failure semantics, replay verified by hashes and provenance, output adapters (`signal-output`: the audio sink, its gain, channel mapping and device conversion), loom models of the scheduler, an independent semantic oracle the engine is tested against, and a multi-GPU device pool with deterministic placement (`signal devices --compute`). Both timing gates passed on the device.
- **M2.8 Web UI foundations** (ADR 0023, 0024, 0025). The `signal-ui` protocol and local server (`signal ui`); web-component controls (fader, knob, 2D input, button, meter, scope) with SVG and pixel skins; panels as project text; Signal.app on a native window with a WebView (wry/tao), with File → Open, Save and Save As, and `.signal` project files (a project folder in one zip; `signal pack`, `signal unpack`). Physical-modelling primitives for sound and visuals (`std.mesh2d`, `std.particles`, `std.body2d`, `<signal-canvas>`), rate divisors and vector ports.
- **M2.9 Schematic and panel editor** (ADR 0026). The signal schematic: an infinite-zoom SVG view of the patch with semantic zoom into composites, live levels and a deterministic layout kept in `ui/schematic.toml`; a panel layout editor; patching from the schematic.
- **M2.10 Core set and built-in library** (ADR 0027). A complete minimal core set of `std.*` primitives by category, a validation suite that rebuilds std modules from core primitives against their native references, and the `lib.*` library of composites shipped with Signal (LFOs, envelopes, oscillators, filters, effects, drum voices; `signal vendor` copies one into a project).
- **M2.11 Studio demo** (ADR 0028). Patterns as project text (`patterns/*.toml`) with live edits, the transport's tick clock, `std.note_seq`, `std.step_seq8`, the `std.ladder` filter, the piano-roll and step-grid controls, and `projects/studio`: a piano roll driving a Moog-style voice through a tape delay over a step-sequenced drum machine with a synthesised 909-style kick.
- **M2.12 Agent interface** (ADR 0029). `signal mcp`: a Model Context Protocol server over the UI protocol, so an LLM client can inspect, play, adjust, patch and render a project. Audio is off unless `--audio`, and the output is held under a gain ceiling (`--max-gain`).
- **M2.13 Schematic path tracing** (ADR 0030). Tracing a signal upstream and downstream through modules and across composite boundaries; composites that open in place with continuous wires; pin notes that read the way the signal flows; labels and text that scale with zoom.
- **M2.14 Release** (ADR 0031). `README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `SECURITY.md`, this changelog, `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` generated from `Cargo.lock`, `scripts/release.sh` and a GitHub Actions release workflow.
- **Several windows over the screens** (ADR 0032, owner's request of 2026-10-22). Signal.app opens any number of main, schematic and panel windows on the running project (Window ▸ New Main Window ⇧⌘N, New Schematic Window, New Panel Window), each on any screen or full screen, with the system's Minimize, Zoom, Bring All to Front and window list. The project panel's cards are moved between windows from their context menu (right-click a card: Move to another window, Move to new panel window, Bring back to main window, Move up / Move down); a main window keeps a placeholder (Show, Bring back) for a card held elsewhere. The layout is kept in the project's `ui/windows.toml` under a key describing the connected screens (names, resolutions, scale, arrangement); another screen configuration opens in the default state (one main window on the main screen with every card), and Window ▸ Reset Layout returns to it. Closing the last window quits as before; closing any other just closes it.
- **Schematic: open on a click, a toolbar** (ADR 0030, owner's requests of 2026-10-22). A composite opens in place on a click and closes with the × in its frame's corner or Escape; zooming and panning only move the view. The schematic's buttons (Edit patch, breadcrumbs, Fit, Reset layout, Reset all) sit on a toolbar row above the drawing instead of floating over it.

### Changed

- Signal is licensed under **GPL-3.0-or-later** (ADR 0031, superseding ADR 0014's provisional "all rights reserved"). Dependencies stay permissive.
- The GUI moved from egui (ADR 0018) to the web UI (ADR 0023).
- Versions count commits: `MAJOR.MINOR.N` (`scripts/version.sh`), built into Signal.app and `signal --version`.

### Known limitations

- Under heavy multi-core load the worst audio callback is about 0.4–0.45 of the period: within the capacity gate, above the 0.25 that holds for single-voice projects.
- `std.tanh_shaper` aliasing on off-divisor tones is below the 60 dB target (V6, waived for M1; planned for M3).
- The app is signed ad hoc, not notarized: the first start needs the Gatekeeper step in the release notes.
- macOS on Apple Silicon only.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-11

M0 and M1: the foundations and the offline core. (Version numbers began later, with the tag `v0.1.0` on 2026-10-16; builds between that tag and `v0.2.0` are `0.1.N`.)

### Added

- **M0 Foundations.** The vision, the inception proposal and ADRs 0001–0015; a Cargo workspace with a pinned toolchain; `AGENTS.md`; the real-time rules; CI (fmt, clippy, tests).
- **M1 Core and offline.** Core types and the module contract (ADR 0002) with its contract test suite, and the execution contract (ADR 0016); the graph crate (composites, flattening, validation, scheduling); TOML project loading and a generated JSON Schema (ADR 0009); the first primitives; the offline driver; `signal render`, `signal modules` and `signal schema`; the test project `projects/kick` and its golden render. Acceptance V1–V5, V7 and V8 pass, V6 is waived until M3, and the owner's listening verdict (V14) is in `experiments/m1-kick-v14/verdict.md`.
