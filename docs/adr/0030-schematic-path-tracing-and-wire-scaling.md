# ADR 0030: Path tracing across composite boundaries, continuous in-place zoom, and wires that scale

- **Status:** Accepted (owner's report of 2026-10-19: "synth moog_voice has about 20 inputs. Inside the structure it is fine, but the outer shell is a wire diagram dead end. We can not trace neither visually, nor in the side panel where the signal goes and comes from. I would suggest a 'scaling' view of the wires, and a fully working path tracing"; then "I like this zooming-in, just let there be a down-scaling wiring — but really I leave it to you, it's fine if it's a bigger task"; design by the operator, delegated). Refines ADR 0026 §2 (the schematic); supersedes nothing.
- **Date:** 2026-10-19

## Context
ADR 0026 promised semantic zoom: "a composite … zoomed in past a threshold, or double-clicked, opens in place and shows its inside". In practice the schematic shows one level at a time: at the top level a composite such as `lib.ladder_voice` or `moog_voice` is a box with about twenty exported ports, and the wires end at its pins; inside, the exported ports appear as boundary pins whose outside is not shown. A signal's path is therefore cut at every composite boundary, both on the canvas and in the sidebar (which lists only the wires at the shown level). With many ports on one box, wires also run together into an unreadable fan.

## Decision
1. **Path tracing, across levels (first).**
   - A *trace* starts from a selected pin, wire or instance and follows connections **upstream, downstream or both** through composite boundaries (exported ports map to the inner ports they forward, both ways), through fan-in and fan-out, and through inserted converters, to the real endpoints: primitive ports, sinks, sources and unconnected ends. It is computed from the `graph` message's structure (as written, every level), so it shows exactly what the project text says; a pure, tested function in `ui/lib/`.
   - **On the canvas:** every wire and pin of the trace at the shown level is highlighted, the rest dimmed; a boundary pin of a closed composite gets a badge summarising what lies behind it ("→ 3 inside: osc1.freq, osc2.freq, osc3.freq"; "← from /melody.hz"), and the composite's box shows which of its ports take part.
   - **In the sidebar:** the trace as a tree by level (top level → composite → inner instance → port), each node clickable to reveal it (zoom and centre, opening composites as needed), with counts and the direction toggle. Escape ends the trace.
   - The trace survives zooming into and out of composites, so the person can walk the path level by level.
2. **Continuous in-place zoom (second).** A composite opens **where it is**: as the view zooms past its threshold (or on double-click), its box grows into a frame that draws its inside at that position, the surrounding level stays visible and dimmed around it, and the outer wires continue across the frame's edge to the inner pins they reach, so a path is one continuous drawing. Zooming out closes it again. Breadcrumbs and the sidebar follow the innermost open level. Layout positions stay per level in `ui/schematic.toml` (ADR 0026), unchanged in format; editing (patching, moving) still happens at one level at a time, the innermost open one.
3. **Wires that scale (with 2).**
   - **Bundles:** at small scale, the wires between the same two boxes (or into one composite) are drawn as one bundle with a count; zooming in fans the bundle out into its wires, smoothly, with a threshold in screen pixels per wire. A traced wire is always drawn on its own, above its bundle.
   - **Port groups on dense boxes:** a box with many ports orders and groups them (by the composite's own export order and name prefixes, e.g. `osc1_*`, `env_*`, or by an optional `group` on an export, a later text addition), and at small scale collapses a group to one labelled pin with a count.
   - Hit testing, hover and selection work on bundles (hover lists the wires; click selects the bundle, a second click a wire).
4. **Protocol:** the `graph` message already carries every level's structure; if the trace needs anything more (e.g. converter detail per connection, export-to-inner mapping made explicit), it is added there, additively, documented in `ui-protocol.md`.
5. **Performance:** the studio (≈180 instances, 20-port boxes) and `library-demo` stay fluid (≥ 50 fps pan/zoom on the Mac); traces of a whole project compute in < 10 ms.

## Refinements (M2.13-2, 2026-10-19)
- **§1, readable boundaries.** The badges of §1 became the pins' labels (measured in their font, kept inside their box, cut short or summarised rather than spilling; a frame's kept on screen): the pins of a closed composite's box and of the frame inside an open one are labelled at a fixed size on screen, never on each other and never left out; where they would collide they are grouped by name prefix, then merged, then counted, and boxes whose summaries collide share one. A frame's pin always says where it connects outside, one connection away (`hz ← /melody.hz`); a box's pin says where it goes inside on hover or when traced; a trace's summary follows the note, never replacing it (`ui/lib/schematic-labels.js`).
- **§1, depth.** A trace goes **to the next module** (as above) or **through modules**: a primitive is crossed from its inputs and parameters to all its outputs (and back upstream), until sinks and sources. This is a structural path, not a signal-dependent one: what a module's values do to its signal is not read.
- **§3, bundles.** "Smoothly" is realised in steps: a pair's bundle fans out between 4 and 8 px per wire on screen in quarter steps, each wire's ends (and a loop's lane) moving from the bundle's to its own. A pair's feedback wires bundle as well, as one loop on the lane of its first. Selecting a bundle selects its wires, which are then drawn on their own; a second click selects the wire nearest the pointer. A dragged box's level is drawn unbundled until the drop.
- **§3, port groups.** Delivered in M2.13-2: a composite's box orders its pins by group (an export's optional `group`, a table form of the export; else its prefix) in the file's export order, which `graph` gives additively as `order` on each export (and `group`); below 11 px a row (a label's pitch, so a pin drawn on its own is always named; at first 8 px, as wires bundle, which left a range of unnamed pins), a group collapses to one pin with its name and count, and its wires join that pin as a bundle.
- **2026-10-20, owner: text scales with zoom; LOD hides instead of keeping constant size; boundary ports named on both sides.** The owner, looking at the schematic: "On the outside and on the inside of the composite, the connecting interface should be named. Right now not every letter scales when zooming — everything should zoom nicely." This replaces the first refinement's "labelled at a fixed size on screen": every text (box titles, pin names, composites' and frames' pin labels and notes, group labels, summaries, bundle counts) is drawn in its level's units inside the SVG and scales with the view as the boxes do. Readability is kept by level of detail, never by a size on screen: every pin named while a group's pins are drawn on their own (11 px a row), else each group named while its text is 4 px or more, else one summary per box (drawn as large as its box holds it), and any text under 4 px is hidden. A frame names **each** exported port on its own, with where it connects outside from 7 px text (`hz ← /melody.hz`); merging and grouping are only the coarser steps, the same on both sides. An open composite's box keeps its pins named outside its edge. Measured in headless Chrome on the studio: 60 fps zooming and panning at the top level and inside the synth, a render at most 11 ms.
- **2026-10-20, owner: a trace goes through the composite.** "Signal passage across composite boundaries still doesn't work; tracing must be able to go through the composite", and of the sidebar of `/synth.gate`: "it does not mention the connections on the inside". A trace of a composite instance now goes through its inside as well; an open composite's box pins are joined to its frame pins by links drawn with the trace, so its highlight crosses the edge; a boundary pin's sidebar lists its other side (Inside / Outside), each a link that goes there, with Go inside / Go outside.
- **§2, delivered in M2.13-3 (2026-10-20).** A composite opens when its inside would be drawn at 0.45 of a layout unit per pixel or more (the readability Enter gives the studio's synth) and closes when a zoom out takes it below 0.375 (the 360/300 px ratio before; 0.28 since the operator's review, below); its box grows where it is into a frame holding the inside at 0.4 of the parent's units, at most 8 times the scale the box holds it at (so the ten-level deep zoom still reaches a total scale of 1e40), never smaller than the box. The frame's scale depends on the layouts only, so a level and its open inside stay one exact link at any zoom. One composite is open per level (a double-clicked one first, else the one open, else the one under the zoom's point or nearest it), so frames never cover each other; the frame lies over its neighbours (moved out of its way since the operator's review, below), the level around is dimmed, and the outer wires are routed to the frame's edge, where the M2.13-2 links carry them to the frame's pins inside. Breadcrumbs are the open levels; patching, moving, undo and redo act at the innermost one only; positions stay the stored layout's, in `ui/schematic.toml` as before. Enter and a breadcrumb never show a level below 0.45 (0.4725 since the review); Fit shows every composite closed (`ui/lib/schematic-open.js`). Constants are tunable at the operator's visual review.
- **2026-10-20, the operator's visual review of §2: make room for the frame.** Headless screenshots of the studio showed the grown synth frame covering its own neighbours (`/melody`, `/echo`, their labels showing through it) and every wire across its edge routed as a loop under the whole level (the frame now enclosed its sources horizontally, so `b.x <= a.x` made them feedback). Decided: the boxes around an open composite are moved out of its frame's way in the drawing only (`makeRoom` in `ui/lib/schematic-open.js`; one shift per side along each axis, so their order and gaps are kept and none overlaps; the stored layout and `ui/schematic.toml` never change); a wire across the edge keeps the direction it ran to the box; CLOSE_K is OPEN_K / 1.6 (0.28; at 0.375 one wheel tick out of Enter's view closed it) and Enter, a double-click and a breadcrumb land 5 % above OPEN_K; a composite opened by a zoom keeps the box's point under the pointer as the same point of its frame, the frame's header below the bar when it fits; only a zoom, a double-click, Enter or a breadcrumb opens a composite, never a pan; the edits of a part around the innermost open level are disabled with the reason, a drag on a box there says "Not moved" instead of panning; a library composite's Open in place is off; a closed composite's pin note that does not fit shows its short form (`hz → 2`) or nothing, never `hz → 2:…`. A level drawn in a frame keeps that frame's size whatever opens inside it (so a level and its inside stay one exact link at any zoom), so the room made there may reach past it. The growth animation stays cut.
- **2026-10-21, owner: "show the signal's path".** A pin's note reads source → destination, its arrow always the way the signal flows: what feeds a pin comes before its name (`/melody.hz → hz`, `2: a.out, b.out → out`, `inside a library composite → x`, `nothing → x`; earlier `hz ← /melody.hz`), what it feeds after (`hz → /echo.in`, `hz → 2: glide.in, hz_prev.in`, unchanged); the short form is `hz → 2` or `2 → out`, else the name alone. The same in the sidebar's lists (`/melody.gate →`), the trace tree's pins (an output `label →`, an input `→ label`) and the trace summaries (an upstream one says "from" without an arrow: `from /melody.hz`).
- **2026-10-22, owner: open on a click, close with ×.** "When I zoom in, a composite opens automatically. It would be better if it opened on a click, and there were an × in the top-right corner to close it." This replaces the zoom thresholds of §2 ("as the view zooms past its threshold … Zooming out closes it again") and of its M2.13-3 delivery (open at 0.45 of a layout unit per pixel, close below 0.28, the one under the zoom's point chosen): a zoom or a pan only moves the view, it never opens or closes a composite. A plain click on a closed composite's box (not on a pin, which keeps selecting, tracing and patching; a drag still moves the box) opens it in place and selects it; a double-click, Enter, Open in place and a reveal open it as before (a double-click and Enter still zoom to a readable view, at least 0.4725). The click does not move the view: the frame grows where the box is, the box's point under the pointer kept as the same point of its frame (as a zoom-opening kept it). Every open frame draws an × at the top right of its header, in its level's units (hidden with any text under 4 px); a click on it, Escape (after a trace's, before the selection's and Edit patch's) or Close closes that composite and everything open inside it, without zooming: the pan its opening by a click made is undone, so the box is back where it was and a click there opens it again. Still one open per level (opening a sibling closes the other and what was open in it); a breadcrumb closes what is open below it, Fit closes everything; library composites stay closed, a click only selects them. What is open stays view state, not project text (`ui/lib/schematic-open.js`: `chooseOpen` takes the pinned set only; `unpin`, `closeBox`).
- **2026-10-22, the operator's review of the open frames.** Five small defects, decided by the operator: the × that closes an open frame is the one drawn element kept at a size on screen (at least 20 px square, its letter about 14.5 px; it was about 12 px at Fit), an exception to "never kept at a size on screen" because it is a control, not text to read, and it is hidden where it would cover the frame's title or not fit in the frame; a forward wire that would pass under an open frame goes around it along a lane outside it (the studio's drum triggers ran under an open voice's corner); a frame's parameter that nothing outside connects to has no note (`nothing → x` stays for a port); a frame's names that would leave the drawing area go inside the frame again (lost when labels moved into the level's units); an open box's noted pin is named whole outside its edge.
- **Not done:** library composites' insides in `graph`: they stay closed (their inside is built by the host, outside a UI-only change); and an animation of the growth (the box becomes its frame in one step at the threshold), cut. For the operator's review on the device: the frame's size and the dimming, screenshots of the synth opening and of a trace across the edge, the frame rate while panning and zooming.

## Alternatives considered
- **A flattened view only:** shows every connection but loses the structure the person wrote; tracing over the structure keeps both.
- **Only tracing, no in-place zoom:** solves the dead end but keeps the path in pieces; the owner likes zooming in, and ADR 0026 already promised in-place opening.

## Consequences
- New pure modules in `ui/lib/` (trace, bundle, port groups) with `node:test` tests; changes to `<signal-schematic>`, its sidebar and its editor; possibly additive fields in `graph`.
- Visual checks in headless Chrome by the operator; the owner judges readability.
